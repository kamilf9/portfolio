scnd

student project | branding | 4 weeks | 2026

about

SCND is a groundbreaking fashion brand based on 3D printing technology. Unlike the common use of this technology for creating avant-garde and unwearable runway pieces, SCND makes technological innovation accessible and wearable for everyday life. The brand offers meticulous, clean, and timeless Ready-to-wear fashion, combining digital precision with high aesthetics.

why

The core problem in the traditional fashion industry is the consumer's need to adapt to generic sizes (S, M, L), which leads to discomfort, negative body image, and a waste of resources (overproduction and waste). At SCND, we flip the equation: instead of you adapting to the garment, we adapt the garment precisely to your measurements. The goal is to leverage the benefits of digital manufacturing—minimal waste and maximum body compatibility—to create more personal, sustainable, and accurate fashion.

how

The connection between technology and the user is established through an intuitive interface bridging the physical and digital experience. This ecosystem features smart fitting rooms with cameras and smart mirrors for precise body scanning, alongside a dedicated app and website that allow users to scan themselves at home. These 3D measurements translate directly into printing files, ensuring a perfect fit and efficient, waste-free production.
