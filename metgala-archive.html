The met gala archive

personal project | ux/ui | 4 weeks | 2025
Explore prototype

about

The Met Gala, also known as “Fashion’s Biggest Night,” is a global phenomenon that has redefined fashion as an art form. I created the first digital archive of its kind to curate and preserve the annual highlights from the event's iconic red carpet. Through digital Curation, this project documents the power of the fashion industry and its influence on our world.
